1. What Unhooked is

Unhooked is an Android app that locks habits you choose to quit (such as pornography, betting, social media or gaming) away on your own phone, for 7 to 365 days that you pay for upfront by M-Pesa. Pornography and betting are blocked on every plan. There is no early exit.

2. What we collect, and why

Your phone's identity, as a one-way hash

A hash of your phone's serial number and IMEI, made on the phone and mixed with a secret on our server. Kept on our server to restore your commitment after a factory reset and to stop a second commitment replacing a live one. Basis: contract.

Your commitment

The habits you chose, its length, time served, which apps are blocked, and its status. Kept on our server and your phone to run, back up and end the commitment. Basis: your explicit consent, and contract.

Your plan payment

The M-Pesa number you enter, the amount, the M-Pesa receipt and the times. Kept on our server to take the payment, start the plan, and refund it if Unhooked cannot deliver. Basis: contract; legal obligation (financial records).

What you agreed to, and a record of each payment change

The terms version, your age confirmation and consent, when you gave them, and each step of the payment. Kept on our server to settle disputes and for audit. Basis: legitimate interest; legal obligation.

Your internet (IP) address

Held in our server's memory for a few minutes, and in our host's logs, to limit abuse and keep the service secure. Basis: legitimate interest.

The habits you choose are sensitive. Choosing to quit pornography, betting, gambling or mobile loans can reveal an addiction or a health condition. We use them only to run your commitment, and we never share them except with our hosting providers. Before you pay, the app asks for your explicit consent to this with a separate tick box, and we record when you gave it.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, messages, call logs, browsing history, location, or the content of your photos and videos.

3. What stays on your phone only

  • Media Shield (pornography plan only) scans photos and videos on your phone. The images are analysed by software on the phone itself and are never uploaded. Files it matches are permanently deleted from the phone. Only a count of deleted files is kept, on the phone.
  • The list of apps on your phone is read on the phone to apply blocking rules. Only the names of apps blocked in your commitment are sent to our server, as part of its backup.
  • The Accessibility service reads which app is open and the text on Settings, uninstall, M-Pesa, My Safaricom, SIM menu and Chrome screens, on the phone, only to stop tampering and payments to betting firms. Nothing it reads is stored or sent anywhere.

4. Who else processes data

  • Safaricom (M-Pesa), to process your payment and any refund: your M-Pesa number and the amount.
  • Our hosting providers, who store and process data for us under contract: Render (application server, Frankfurt, Germany) and Turso (database).
  • CleanBrowsing, and Cloudflare as backup, which provide the family-safe DNS filter. While a commitment is live, your phone asks them for the address of every website and app service it connects to. Their own privacy policies apply. We never receive these requests.
  • Google, because Unhooked uses Android's Factory Reset Protection with Unhooked's own Google accounts, and sets Chrome's SafeSearch and adult-site filter. We send no data about you to Google.
  • Hugging Face, from which the phone downloads Media Shield's models, and GitHub and Vercel, which serve the app download and this website. They see your internet address when your phone downloads from them.
  • Authorities, only where Kenyan law requires it.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. Transfers outside Kenya

Our server and database are hosted outside Kenya (Germany and other regions of our providers). We transfer data only to providers bound by contract to protect it, as the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Commitment records: for the life of the commitment and 12 months after it ends.
  • Payment records and what you agreed to: 7 years, as financial records.
  • Phone hashes: until 12 months after your last commitment ends.
  • IP addresses: minutes in our server's memory; our host's logs for a short period.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. Contact us at jacotewambura@gmail.com.

While a commitment is live, deleting its record would let a factory reset end it early, which is the one thing you paid us to prevent. If you ask us to delete it during a commitment, we delete it when the commitment ends.

8. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Phone identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes. Payment credentials stay on our server and are never placed in the app. App updates are installed only if they are signed with our key.

9. Children

Unhooked is for adults (18 or over). We do not knowingly provide it to children.

10. Changes

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect. The version date is at the top of this page.
